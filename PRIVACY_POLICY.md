## Overview

Film Photography Viewfinder ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party. All data created while using the App is stored exclusively on your device.

---

## Data We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information. All data is created and stored locally on your device only, and includes:

| Data type | Purpose | Where stored |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Log book entries (shot records) | Track exposures and film rolls | On-device (local storage) |
| Custom film formats and lenses | Personalise the viewfinder | On-device (local storage) |
| App settings and preferences | Remember your configuration | On-device (local storage) |
| Film stock library customisations | Custom film stock entries | On-device (local storage) |

No data is ever uploaded to a server, shared with third parties, or used for analytics or advertising.

---

## Device Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Camera** | Live viewfinder preview and light metering. Images are not saved without your explicit action and are never transmitted off-device. |
| **Microphone** | Countdown timer audio feedback only. No audio is recorded or stored. |
| **Photo Library (write)** | Save captured photos to your camera roll, only when you explicitly trigger a save. |

---

## Data Retention

All data produced by the App (log entries, formats, lenses, settings) is stored locally on your device for **as long as the App is installed**. The App does not store any data on external servers, so the developer holds no user data and has no retention policy beyond what you control on your own device.

---

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control of deletion:

### Delete all App data
- **iOS:** Go to *Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Film Photography Viewfinder → Delete App*. This removes the App and all associated data permanently.
- **Android:** Go to *Settings → Apps → Film Photography Viewfinder → Storage → Clear Data*, or uninstall the App to remove all data.

### Delete specific data within the App
- **Log book entries:** Open the Log Book tab, swipe left on any entry and tap Delete.
- **Custom film formats / lenses:** Open Settings → Formats & Lenses, select the item, and tap Delete.
- **App settings / preferences:** Uninstalling and reinstalling the App resets all preferences to defaults.

### Request deletion by email
If you have any concerns about data held on your device, you can contact the developer at [jonassubvert@gmail.com](mailto:jonassubvert@gmail.com) and we will assist you within **30 days**.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or user tracking. There are no third-party services with access to your data.

## Security

All data is stored using your device's standard local storage (AsyncStorage on Android and iOS). Because no data leaves your device and no account or network connection is involved, there is no server-side security risk. Your device's own lock screen and encryption protect the data at rest.

## Your Rights (GDPR & applicable law)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or another jurisdiction with data protection laws, you have the right to:

- **Access** — know what data the App holds about you (all of it is on your device, visible within the App)
- **Rectification** — correct any inaccurate data (editable directly within the App)
- **Erasure** — delete your data at any time (see *Data Deletion* above)
- **Portability** — export your data (log entries and formats are editable/visible in-app)
- **Object** — since no data is processed for any commercial purpose, there is nothing to object to

Because no personal data is transmitted to the developer, these rights are exercised directly on your device. If you need assistance, contact [jonassubvert@gmail.com](mailto:jonassubvert@gmail.com).
